Husband and wife. Chef duo. Two decades of excellence.

Claire & Marc Lara

Their culinary journey has taken them across the globe, serving celebrities, athletes, and royalty — earning them a renowned reputation for excellence and discretion.

Claire Lara made history in 2010 as the first woman to win MasterChef: The Professionals. A standout moment came when she cooked for the late Queen and Prince Philip, and she has since collaborated with renowned chefs including Mary Berry during the King's coronation celebrations.

Every dish they craft is a celebration of artistry and taste, infused with a personalised touch that ensures each dining experience is truly unique. Passionate about sourcing seasonal, high-quality ingredients, they apply refined techniques to create menus as exquisite as they are memorable.
